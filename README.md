## 1. OVERVIEW & AUTONOMOUS STRATEGY
RestoHunter AI is an autonomous B2B client acquisition system built to replace a 5–6 person sales and marketing agency.

Instead of sending generic "hire me" pitches, the system:
1. Takes a single input: **`TARGET_CITY`** (e.g., Austin, Miami, London, Toronto, Sydney, New York).
2. Scans profitable local businesses with active Google Business profiles (**4.0+ Stars, 30+ Reviews**).
3. Diagnoses high-ticket digital flaws:
   - Pinch-to-zoom PDF menus costing $2,800–$5,200/mo in DoorDash 30% commission cuts.
   - Missing after-hours online patient booking calendars costing $5,000+/mo in emergency consultations.
   - Phone-only booking with high no-show rates ($1,500–$3,000/mo lost).
   - 2010-era non-responsive sites lacking instant quote calculators ($10,000+ lost jobs).
   - Boutique hotel OTA commission leaks (18–25% to Booking.com/Expedia).
4. Generates hyper-personalized pitches with live interactive demo concepts.
5. Dispatches outreach across ban-proof channels with randomized 3 to 7 minute human interval spacing.
6. Tracks Day-3 follow-ups automatically and pushes real-time reply alerts directly to your WhatsApp.

## 3. MULTI-NICHE TARGETS & FINANCIAL FLAW AUDITS

### 🍽️ 1. Restaurants, Cafes & Pizzerias
* **Flaw:** PDF Menu on mobile (pinch-to-zoom nightmare), no direct mobile ordering.
* **Financial Leak:** DoorDash/UberEats 30% commission cut ($2,800 – $5,200/month lost).
* **Pitch Angle:** 1-tap mobile ordering preview link with zero commission.

### 🩺 2. Dental & Medical Clinics
* **Flaw:** No after-hours online patient booking calendar, paper/PDF intake forms.
* **Financial Leak:** Lost emergency consultations and after-hours bookings ($5,000+/month).
* **Pitch Angle:** 24/7 automated booking calendar & paperless patient intake.

### ✂️ 3. Barber Shops, Salons & Spas
* **Flaw:** Manual phone booking only, no automated SMS/WhatsApp reminders.
* **Financial Leak:** High no-show rates ($1,500 – $3,000/month lost).
* **Pitch Angle:** Mobile booking widget with zero-no-show automated reminders.

### 🔨 4. Home Services (Roofing, Emergency Plumbing, HVAC)
* **Flaw:** 2010-era non-responsive website, missing instant quote calculator & 24/7 emergency call button.
* **Financial Leak:** Losing $10,000+ roofing/remodeling jobs to competitors ranking in Google local pack.
* **Pitch Angle:** High-converting landing page with 1-click quote inquiry.

### 🏨 5. Boutique Hotels, Resorts & Motels
* **Flaw:** Outdated room photo galleries, no direct booking engine.
* **Financial Leak:** 18–25% commission lost to Booking.com and Expedia ($4,000 – $9,800/month).
* **Pitch Angle:** Direct commission-free room reservation engine.
